The Symbolism of Light Over Darkness

✨

Diwali’s primary symbolism lies in the victory of light over darkness.

Lighting diyas signifies the removal of negativity and ignorance, filling lives with hope and wisdom. This light symbolizes inner peace and knowledge, encouraging individuals to embrace compassion, kindness, and humility.

SACRED EMBLEMS

Core Symbols of Diwali

🌺
🪔

Diwali is celebrated with symbolic items like diyas, rangoli, sweets, and garlands.

Diyas, central to Diwali decor, symbolize purity and enlightenment, while rangoli designs welcome guests and deities alike. Sweets are shared among family and friends, embodying the joy of togetherness and prosperity.

CELEBRATION IN DETAIL

Diyas (Oil Lamps), Rangoli, Flowers, Sweets

“Diyas, or oil lamps, are lit in homes and temples to create an atmosphere of warmth and positivity. Rangoli, often made with coloured powders or flowers, adorns doorsteps and signifies luck and beauty. Flowers decorate puja spaces, and sweets are exchanged to spread joy and friendship during Diwali.”

✨
🪔 Oil Lamps

Diyas (Oil Lamps)

Symbol of Purity, Hope & Enlightenment

Diyas, or oil lamps, are lit in homes and temples to create an atmosphere of warmth and positivity. Central to Diwali decor, they dispel shadows and represent the eternal light dwelling in every heart.

🎨 Sacred Art

Rangoli

Symbol of Luck, Harmony & Welcome

Rangoli, often made with coloured powders or flowers, adorns doorsteps and signifies luck and beauty. Intricate symmetrical patterns welcome guests, deities, and radiant cosmic harmony into the household.

🌸
🌺 Auspicious Decor

Flowers & Garlands

Symbol of Beauty, Freshness & Devotion

Flowers decorate puja spaces and entrance torans to spread fragrant freshness and grace. Marigold garlands and sacred jasmine embody divine blessings, purity, and festive celebration.

🍬 Mithai Feasts

Sweets (Mithai)

Symbol of Sweetness, Generosity & Friendship

Sweets are exchanged to spread joy and friendship during Diwali. From motichoor laddoos to silver-leaf kaju katli, sharing delicacies embodies the sweetness of togetherness, goodwill, and mutual prosperity.
